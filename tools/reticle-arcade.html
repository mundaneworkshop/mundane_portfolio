Move the cursor: the grids parallax with its speed and acceleration, and settle when it stops. Click to fire. H = simulate a hit. Hit a drifting target to see the real thing.

Arcade reticle

Oval = crosshair. The reticle runs at a low, stepped frame rate (8 fps by default) like the CRT displays in the films; set the slider to 60 for smooth. The two perspective grids show turn angle: they recede toward the oval (the convergence slider sets how much), the direction of the cursor yaws / tilts them, and the harder you steer the closer they swing to edge-on. Everything below is tunable.
