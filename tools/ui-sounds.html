Site Sound Review

The sounds that belong to the whole site, not the arcade: the site-wide ambient bed, the entry gate, dock, boot and planet-load events, and the interface sounds. Each card lists the clips the site plays today (served from assets/audio/, so open this page through a local server); drop new candidates onto it to audition them and write notes. Candidates and notes stay in this browser (this page has no database). The arcade's own sounds and ships live in the Arcade Fleet Lab artifact.

Interface sounds (synthesised)

Mechanical, modelled on measurements of two reference reels (switches, buttons, camera shutter, clicks). Click, hold, release and hover to hear each sound. They are synthesised live (no files), so every press varies slightly. Sounds start after your first click.

The approved switch design (Figma 12 · Toggle) with its sound states. Hover for the tick, press and hold for the press-in, release to latch. Space or Enter latches without the press-in. Disabled is silent and never hovers. Pick a recipe, then compare.
