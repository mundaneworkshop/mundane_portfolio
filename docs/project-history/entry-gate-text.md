# Entry gate text + hold-then-dock (Oct 22 2026)

> **UPDATE (Oct 22 2026, `feat/gate-text-marquee`): the DOM H1 + two tilted H2 lines described below are GONE.** They are replaced by two 3D marquee bands that float around the monogram cube. Everything below about `#gate`, `#gateTitle`, `.gate-h2`, the crawl planes, the exit mask, `--gate-*` custom properties and the old "Entry gate · text" debug controls is historical. What still applies: the settle detection (`gateLayout`), the hold-then-dock timing, the monogram intro and hover, and the hit circle.

## Marquee bands (current)
- **What**: the H1 ("MUNDANE WORKSHOP", Chakra Petch 600) is one looping band of text on a rounded-square loop around the cube's **Y axis**; the H2 (Space Grotesk 500) is a second band around the cube's **X axis**, so the two loops are perpendicular. Each band is a ribbon that stands off the cube faces it crosses by `params.gateLift` (% of the cube's half-side, default 18; the H2 band floats a little higher so the two never touch where they cross). Text is on the outside, reads left to right as seen from outside, and scrolls round the loop (`params.gateSpeed`, % of a loop per second, default 6; H1 and H2 run opposite ways). All text is upper-cased; a small square separates repeats.
- **H2 copy**: both old lines ("The creative practice of product designer Jason Hsin", "Building delightful digital consumer experiences") live on the one H2 band and swap each time it completes a loop (`params.gateH2Alt`). Two sentences at once on a single loop were illegible at the cube's on-screen size.
- **How (code: "Entry gate bands" in the monogram script, `gbBuild`/`gateBandsFrame`)**: the group rides `_mwM`, the same monogram-space to world matrix the studio impostor quad uses, so the bands spin and tilt rigidly with the cube. The cube is axis-aligned in monogram space with half-side `mwMono.radius / sqrt(3)` (its bounding sphere is the cube's circumsphere), so no pose maths of its own. The monogram itself is only a flat billboard, so a **colour-less box of the cube's size** (`colorWrite:false, depthWrite:true`, `renderOrder` 40, after the quad) writes depth and the bands (`renderOrder` 41, `depthWrite:false`) are occluded by it: the far half of each loop is hidden, the near half passes in front. Verified against the real render: the loops tuck behind the cube silhouette where they should.
- **Texture**: each text is drawn once into a canvas tile (text + gap + square + gap), white, and tinted by the material colour (`--holo`, re-tinted by a `MutationObserver` on theme changes). The tile repeats an integer number of times round the loop so the marquee wraps with no seam; the band height follows from that (the smallest of the lines' heights wins). Scrolling is `texture.offset.x`.
- **Timing**: same settle trigger as before (centre and radius still for 150 ms, 4 s fallback). H1 band fades in at the settle (`gateH1Delay` 0), H2 band `gateH2Delay` (1000 ms) later, each over `gateIn` (2000 ms, smoothstep). On click `startDock` fades both out over `gateFade` (380 ms) while the monogram holds (`dockHold` 0.7 s), then the dock runs as before. `prefers-reduced-motion`: no scrolling, no line swapping, opacity fades only.
- **Tunables** (Debug panel, "Entry gate · text bands"): float height, band height (`gateBandH`, % of cube side, default 16), scroll speed, H2 alternation, fade-in, fade-out, H1/H2 delays. Lift / band height / alternation rebuild the geometry.
- **Gotchas**: the bands go through the same pixelate + posterize pass as the scene (the rt is 1/`params.pixel` size), so text is blocky; keep it big. Fonts must be loaded before the tiles are drawn (`document.fonts.load`, done inside `gateBandsFrame`). Phone layout is not tuned (mobile gets its own pass). To change which cube axes the loops use, edit the `axis` fields of `GB_BANDS`.

> The entry gate (monogram on a starfield, before the first click) carries the site's one-line intro. Clicking the monogram now HOLDS (the monogram keeps spinning) for 0.7 s while the text leaves, then runs the dock itself at 0.3 s (2x the original 0.6 s speed).

## What's on screen
- **H1** `MUNDANE WORKSHOP` above the monogram (`#gateTitle`, Chakra Petch, holo teal). It has no hover effect: the Aurebesh decode that briefly lived on it was removed (too much).
- **Two H2 lines** below it (`.gate-h2`), "The creative practice of product designer Jason Hsin" / "Building delightful digital consumer experiences", on one tilted plane (`.gate-plane`: `rotateX(--gate-tilt)`, default 34 deg, origin at its top edge, parent `perspective: --gate-persp`, 520 px) for the Star Wars crawl convergence. 52 deg / 320 px was too extreme to read. They are children of the same plane, so rise/exit travel happens in plane space.
- Position (window-anchored, responsive): the text does NOT follow the monogram. The H1's bottom edge sits at `--gate-h1-y` (23% of window height) and the crawl's bottom at `--gate-crawl-b` (8%), so camera pan/zoom never moves them. Font sizes use `vw`/`vh` clamps (`min(3.2vw,5.4vh)` for the H1, `min(2.2vw,3.4vh)` for the H2), with a phone breakpoint (<700 px: H2 wraps, balanced, smaller) and a short-landscape tweak (H1 at 20%, crawl at 5%). `gateLayout(sW,dist,c)` still projects the monogram's centre and radius each frame, but only to write the mask (`--gate-cx/cy/rx/ry`, radius x0.95) and the exit distances (`--gate-h1-fly`, `--gate-exit`), which are computed from the real DOM layout (`offsetTop`) so they stay correct at any size; a `resize` listener sets `__gateDirty`. When camera zoom/pan makes the mark bigger, it covers the text because the mask is always on.

## Timing
- **Fade-in is staggered and waits for the camera, with no input needed.** Settle = the monogram's projected centre and radius have drifted less than `GATE_STILL_PX` (1.5 px) from where they were `GATE_SETTLE_MS` (150 ms) ago, measured in wall-clock time (a slow easing tail counts as still), with a `GATE_MAX_WAIT_MS` (4 s) fallback. Then `body.mw-gate-on` starts CSS transitions with delays from that moment: H1 immediately (`--gate-h1-delay` 0), the first H2 line 1000 ms after the settle (`--gate-h2-delay` 1000), the second H2 line 500 ms after that. Measured in a browser: the text is released about 0.1 s after the last real movement and the H1 starts its fade straight away (at the original 700 ms H1 delay it reached 10% opacity 1.2 s after the settle). Each fade is 2000 ms (`--gate-in`): opacity on a gentle in-out S-curve `cubic-bezier(.45,0,.15,1)` (50% about 0.75 s in, 95% about 1.5 s; an expo-out looked like a quick pop with a long tail even at 2 s), the 46 px rise on expo-out `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` for a soft landing (plane space for the H2 lines).
  - **Why the first version took ~3 s instead of 1 s:** it tested the QUAD's screen box for stillness, and that box changes by a pixel or two every frame as the mark spins on its corner, so it never counted as still and the 3 s fallback timer always won. Settle must be measured on spin-independent quantities (centre, radius).
- **Click** (`startDock`): `mw-gate-on` -> `mw-gate-out`. Both layers CRAWL BEHIND the monogram on angled planes, receding toward it: the H2 lines up their plane (`translateY(-var(--gate-exit))`, origin at the plane's top), the H1 down a mirrored plane (`rotateX(-tilt) translateY(var(--gate-h1-fly))` inside `#gateTop`, which has its own `perspective` with `perspective-origin: 50% 100%` and the H1 `transform-origin: 50% 100%`, so it vanishes toward the mark below it); both distances are divided by `cos(tilt)` so the on-screen travel lands inside the mask. The transforms run `--gate-out` (600 ms) on an accelerating curve `cubic-bezier(.5,0,.85,.45)`, while opacity finishes in `--gate-fade` (380 ms, `cubic-bezier(.3,0,.6,1)`), so each line is invisible while still about 130-160 px short of the middle of the mark (computed from the curves at 380/600 ms), and the always-on elliptical mask around the monogram (`--gate-cx/cy/rx/ry`) hides whatever is left as it passes behind it.
- **Hold, then dock.** `startDock` only schedules the dock (`_dockAt = now + params.dockHold`, default 0.7 s, must be >= the 600 ms exit); the phase stays `entry` so the monogram keeps spinning. `mwUpdate` then calls `beginDock()` and the dock runs for `params.dockDur` (default 0.30 s; 0.60 s originally, briefly 3 s). `mw-entrygate` is removed and the boot cascade starts when it completes. Measured: text gone at about 0.6 s, dock starts about 0.7 s, docked about 1.0-1.1 s.
- Only `transform` and `opacity` animate (plus the mask, and the monogram's scale/opacity in its intro). `prefers-reduced-motion`: opacity only, no travel, no stagger delay.

## Around the gate
- **Monogram intro.** The first frame the monogram renders (`mwState==='ready'`, entry phase), `mwUpdate` runs `introK` for `params.mwIntroDur` (0.7 s, ease-out cubic): the mark grows from 55% of its size and its opacity goes 0 -> 1 (`sizeMul` and `mwQuad.material.opacity`), instead of popping in. Measured: opacity 0.54 at 110 ms, 0.96 at 400 ms. The gate text's settle detection simply waits for this growth to finish (the radius is part of the settle test).
- **No nav, no hint on the gate.** `body.mw-entrygate #breadcrumb` is `opacity:0; pointer-events:none` (it fades in when the dock completes and the class is removed); the old bottom `#entryHint` ("drag to orbit...") is gone entirely (the camera can still be orbited and zoomed).
- **"It needs a click" (resolved).** Nothing in the gate depends on a click or any input; the only listener is the monogram click that starts the dock. The sequence is driven by the first RENDERED frame, so in a hidden / background preview pane (which renders nothing until something interacts with it) it only starts when the pane is woken. In a normal Chrome tab it starts by itself; the remaining complaint there was the 3 s gap above, now fixed.

## Monogram hover (entry gate)
The old hover framed the monogram with the targeting reticle snapped to its bounding box (the quad's box: far larger than the mark, so its corners ran into the H1 and the H2 lines). Replaced by a hover on the mark itself; the reticle no longer targets the entry monogram (`targets()` returns null in the entry phase), so the idle reticle just follows the pointer and the canvas cursor stays `default` (no pointer hand, no label).
- **Hit area** (`overMonogram(e)`): a circle around the mark (the projected centre and `radius * sW` that `gateLayout` writes to `_mwHit` every frame, times 0.92), used for BOTH hover and the click that starts the dock, so the empty corners of the old square quad no longer count. The pointer-move handler passes its own event (the canvas handler runs before the window handler that updates `mouse`).
- **Reaction** (`mwHoverK`, 0..1, eased at rate 9/s in `mwUpdate`; it holds during the pre-dock hold and drops once the dock moves or the pointer leaves the window): the mark scales up 6% (the quad only, so the text layout does not move), its spin slows by 30% (`_logoSpin` is accumulated instead of `t*0.35*spin`, identical when not hovered), its opacity gets a faint holo flicker (8%), and it GLOWS. The glow must come from the mark's own geometry (a separate halo sprite behind it was tried and rejected): the entry quad's material is patched (`onBeforeCompile`, in `mwLoad`) to read the mark's silhouette, the alpha of its studio render, and add in holo teal, scaled by `uGlow` (= hover amount): (a) a rim light along its edges, (b) a soft halo that follows the outline, including into the gaps between its parts, (c) a lift on its brightest facets. The quad is drawn `mwPadU` (1.4x) larger with the texture re-centred (`uPad`) so the halo has room outside the mark; with `uGlow` 0 it renders identically to the plain textured quad. The pixel + posterize pass bands the glow into steps, which suits the site.
- **Tunables** (Debug panel, Entry gate): scale-up (`mwHoverScale` 0.06), spin slowdown (`mwHoverSpin` 0.3), glow (`mwHoverGlow` 0.45), flicker (`mwHoverFlicker` 0.08).
